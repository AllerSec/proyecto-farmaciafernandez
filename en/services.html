Our services

Everything you need for your health and wellbeing, with personalised pharmaceutical care in Bera, Navarra.

Comprehensive care

A complete pharmacy
at your service

At Farmacia Fernández Díez we offer a wide range of pharmaceutical and parapharmacy services to cover all your health needs.

Medication dispensing

Dispensing of prescription and over-the-counter medications. We explain dosage, interactions and storage to ensure the correct and safe use of your treatment.

Pharmaceutical advice

Personalised care to answer your questions about medications, minor symptoms and healthy habits. Our team listens and guides you without an appointment.

Dermo-cosmetics

Selection of skincare, hair and body products from the best brands: Avène, La Roche-Posay, Eucerin, Uriage and more. Personalised advice included.

Blood pressure monitoring

Free blood pressure measurement. We offer personalised follow-up and record-keeping for your cardiovascular health, no appointment needed.

Orthopaedics & medical supplies

Wide range of orthopaedic products: back braces, wrist supports, knee braces, compression stockings, wound care and first aid materials.

Natural products & herbal medicine

Selection of medicinal plants, herbal teas, food supplements, vitamins and herbal medicine products for natural wellbeing.

Vaccines (outside official schedule)

Dispensing of vaccines not included in the official vaccination schedule. Please ask us about availability and requirements.

Compounded medications

Preparation of customised pharmaceutical compounds according to medical prescription. Formulas tailored to each patient's specific needs.

Herbal Medicine & Wellness

Nature in the service
of your health

Medicinal plants, natural products and herbal medicine selected with pharmaceutical expertise to complement and strengthen your wellbeing.
