Cookie Policy

Last updated: 18 April 2026

1. What are cookies?

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores in the User's browser when visited. Cookies allow websites to recognise the User on subsequent visits, remember their preferences or collect statistical information about site usage.

2. Types of cookies used

This website uses the following categories of cookies:

  • Strictly necessary or technical cookies: essential for providing the service expressly requested by the User (e.g. remembering the cookie acceptance/rejection choice). These are exempt from prior consent.
  • Analytics cookies: allow us to count visitors and statistically analyse the use of the site. These require the User's prior consent.

3. Cookies used on this site

Name Provider Purpose Duration
_ga Google Analytics 4 (via Google Tag Manager) Anonymously identify users to compute site usage statistics. 2 years
_ga_<ID> Google Analytics 4 Persist analytics session state for the measurement property. 2 years
_gid Google Analytics Distinguish anonymous users. Only set when the property uses Universal Analytics as a fallback. 24 hours

4. Consent

When the User first accesses the Site, an informational banner is displayed allowing them to accept or reject the installation of analytics cookies. Until consent is given, no third-party cookies other than strictly necessary ones will be loaded.

5. Managing and withdrawing consent

The User can modify or withdraw consent at any time from their browser settings. The main browsers allow you to:

  • Block all cookies or only third-party cookies.
  • Delete previously stored cookies.
  • Set warnings before installing new cookies.

Help links for the main browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Disabling cookies may affect the proper functioning of some features of the site.

6. International transfers

The use of Google Analytics, managed by Google LLC, may involve international data transfers to the United States. Such transfers are covered by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, as well as the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

7. Modifications

This Policy may be modified depending on legislative, technical or tool developments. We recommend that the User periodically review this page.
