Our story

Committed to
your health

Farmacia Fernández Díez is a community pharmacy located at the heart of Bera, at Plaza de los Fueros, 4. Under the ownership of Arantza Fernández Díez, we have built a relationship of trust with the families of Bera and its surroundings.

Our commitment goes beyond dispensing medications. We are a reference point for community health, offering professional pharmaceutical advice, follow-up services and close personal attention that makes a real difference.

We believe in the pharmacy as an essential, accessible and human public service. Every person who walks through our door receives individualised attention, because every health situation is unique.
