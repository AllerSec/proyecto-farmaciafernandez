Who we are

A pharmacy for the community

Farmacia Fernández Díez has long been the health reference point for Bera and its surroundings. Under the ownership of Arantza Fernández Díez, we offer close, professional and personalised pharmaceutical care.

More than a pharmacy, we are a community health service where every person receives the attention and time they deserve. We listen, advise and accompany you in the care of your health.
